ABOUT ME

Hi! I'm Giulia Coviello, a creative soul with a unique and unconventional touch, constantly driven to experiment with new ideas and media. I've had a deep passion for art since I was a child, which I express by exploring different visual languages, colors, and communication tools. I'm constantly looking for new ways to express myself, explore my creativity and develop my skills. What excites me most is cross-disciplinary experimentation: I love blending art with code to create interactive works, designing and editing audio tracks, and leveraging 3D graphics for augmented and virtual reality.

For me, creativity is about observing the world from different perspectives, being curious and finding beauty in the smallest details.

PROJECT 04

The three-dimensional sculpture explores the theme of suicide.

PROJECT 05

re-sound

The editing is based on the concept of "truthful dubbing": it utilizes realistic and authentic sounds, enriched with environmental and "disturbing" sound effects, maintaining a millimetric synchronization between audio and image to amplify the emotional journey.

It explores childhood memories, nature, and the passage of time through nostalgic videos and aged locations (such as old family homes or parks), blending the joy of innocence with the melancholy of time's fleeting nature.

4 videos

PROJECT 06

Particles

The visual and sound realization takes shape through a well-defined multimedia workflow: 3D modeling and particle animation were developed in Blender, the creation, processing, and sound design of the audio tracks were handled in Adobe Audition, while the editing and finalization of the audiovisual work were completed in Adobe Premiere Pro.

The particles represent partisans, units of resistance in a world dominated by darkness and oppression. Light symbolizes struggle and hope: every movement is an act of resistance that, uniting with the others, forms an indomitable collective force.

4 videos

PROJECT 12

Body modification - Edited and sound design

Body modification as a subversive practice: rejecting standardized beauty norms through transforming the body 'for the worse' in performance art.

The body is no longer viewed as a temple to idealize or a surface to conform to societal standards, but rather as a political territory, living matter, and a tool for radical protest. Through extreme modification and performance art, the body is deliberately stripped of consumerist aesthetics and polished perfection, becoming a vehicle for unease and uncomfortable truths.

PROJECT 14

Interactive experience, responsive layout.

The work explores and makes tangible the concept of the four dimensions through a computer vision system based on hand-tracking via the device's camera.

The audience interacts with the installation by configuring their hands into specific gestures, which directly act upon the spatiotemporal parameters of the experience: A open hand activates and defines depth. A closed fist and an open hand regulate width. Two open hands control height. A closed fist acts as a stop command, freezing the fourth dimension (time).

Interactive model.

PROJECT 15

gif, Infinite Circle

It is an hypnotic visual artwork that explores the symbolism of elementary geometry, placing the circle at its core and contrasting formal perfection with provocative, cutting phrases.

Through fluid variations in scale, color, and perspective, the circle unfolds in a continuous loop that evokes absolute cyclicity, wrapping the viewer in a vortex of pure aesthetic ecstasy.

Leveraging digital animation and immersive projections, the installation blends light, synchronized movement, and contrasting texts to suspend the perception of space and time, plunging the audience into the infinite essence of the form.
